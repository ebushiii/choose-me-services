Privacy Policy

Last updated October 27, 2026

This policy explains what Choose Me Services ("we") collects through choosemeservices.com, how it's used, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • If you email or call us, we keep what you send (your name, contact details and what you need notarized) to answer you and schedule the appointment.
  • Appointments are booked on Square's booking page. Square collects what you enter there under its own privacy policy.

Like every website, our host receives standard technical information when you visit (such as your IP address and browser type) to deliver and secure the site.

How we use it

Only to answer you, deliver what you asked for, run the site, and keep it secure. We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Who helps us

We use these service providers, who handle your information only to do that job for us:

  • Square: appointment booking and payments
  • OpenStreetMap: the service-area map (map tiles are loaded from their servers)

Cookies and tracking

This site doesn't use advertising pixels or third-party tracking cookies.

Children

This site isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you think a child has sent us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us what we have about you, to correct it, or to delete it, and you can unsubscribe from any email at any time. Contact us at choosemeservices@gmail.com or (708) 918-3335. We won't treat you differently for asking. California residents have these rights under California law, and we honor them for everyone.

How long we keep it, and security

We keep messages and records only as long as needed for the purpose you gave them to us, or as the law requires, then delete them. Access is limited to the people who need it, and all traffic to this site is encrypted.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date above.
